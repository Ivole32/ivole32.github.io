Privacy

Last updated: 2026-05-15

This site is intentionally lightweight and privacy-first. No cookies are set, no external trackers are loaded, and no personal data is actively collected.

No cookies, no tracking

  • No cookies or local tracking scripts.
  • No Google Analytics, ad networks, or pixels.
  • No externally loaded fonts or libraries.

Locally hosted assets

All styles, scripts, and assets are served locally. There are no third-party requests required for rendering.

Server logs

When you visit, the hosting provider may process technically necessary connection data (e.g., IP address, timestamp, user agent). We do not analyze this data.
